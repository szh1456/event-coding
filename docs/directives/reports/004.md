## 5. Deviations and incidents

1. **Rows that directive 003 skipped** were passed with `d_start = None`.
   `evaluate_affine` skips them before the fit, by the same tests as
   `ec.regen.evaluate_regen`, so the value is never read. Acceptance check 3
   confirms that every such row skipped here with the same reason.
2. **Reducer dry run.** Before it was committed, `scripts/af_reduce.py` was copied
   twice to the host mirror and run on the first 13 to 15 shards, into a scratch
   directory that was then deleted. The first copy crashed on an empty selection
   (Table 5 for night, which had no rows yet). The guard is in the committed
   reducer. No running worker used that file, and the final reduction ran after a
   regular sync at `7087567`.
3. **Cost.** 84.4 CPU-hours against a projection of 58.9, 1.43 times. As in 003, the
   probe sampled no instant with a box over 100,000 px². Within the limit of 150.
4. **Choices where the directive is silent,** fixed before any number:
   - The growth stratum uses the interpolated label box (`Track.box`) at `t_e` and at
     `t_e + 300,000 us`.
   - Table 2's stretch quantiles are over pooled rows of stratum H; the median over
     recordings is in the JSON. The share with a nonzero shape parameter is a
     per-recording mean, then the median over recordings, with the pooled share next
     to it.
   - Table 5 reports pooled rows, as asked, and each lighting separately.
   - Step 1 of the rule is read on the median over recordings of the per-instant
     ratio, as Section 6 forms ratios per instant.
5. **Shards and event slices,** as in directives 002 and 003. Acceptance check 4
   (exact `translation` against directive 003) confirms that results are unchanged.

## 6. Questions for the director

1. **Step 1 is decided by night alone.** By day the ratio at K = 11 is 1.156
   [1.127, 1.173], above 1.10. By night it is 1.082 [1.068, 1.093], whose upper bound
   is below 1.10. The day/night difference is also in Table 4: fast vehicles gain
   1.39 by day and 1.18 by night. Is the paper's ablation stated per lighting?
2. **Where the affine map pays.** For vehicles above 100 px/s, the ratio at K = 11 is
   1.14 to 1.39. Below 100 px/s, for all classes, it is 1.02 to 1.12. A codec that
   sends shape parameters only for fast objects would keep most of the gain for a
   fraction of the 48 bits. That cost is negligible anyway (Table 6), so is the
   question only one of the decision rule?
3. **Rotation and shear.** `ib` and `ic` are within ±3 px at m = 10 for 80% of rows.
   The stretch parameters `ia` and `id` reach ±7 and ±5. If the paper keeps a
   subset, a two-parameter scale (`ia`, `id`) is the candidate the data point to.
