This directive has no decision rule (Section 7). The findings, in stratum H at the
headline cell (2 px, 11.1 ms):

- **Placement, not counts, carries the loss.** The regenerated windows hold nearly
  the right number of events: the count ceiling of `aligned` is 0.986 (day) and 0.985
  (night) at m = 1 and 0.915 and 0.921 at m = 10. The placement of `affine` falls
  from 0.696 and 0.722 to 0.579 and 0.582. In the exact log split
  (F1 = ceiling x placement), the ceiling accounts for 0.28 (day) and 0.26 (night) of
  the fall of `F1(affine)` from m = 1 to 10.
- **Counts follow the growth of the box.** At m = 10, the median `rho` = n_true /
  n_source is 0.67 / 0.70 for boxes that shrink below 0.8 and 1.61 / 1.45 for boxes
  that grow beyond 1.25.
- **The loss follows the lead, and also varies with the distance, the other way
  round.** At a fixed displacement of 5 to 10 px, the placement of `affine` falls by
  0.23 (day) and 0.18 (night) from m = 1 to m = 10. At fixed m = 10 it does not stay
  flat across displacements from 2 to 40 px. It *rises* with the displacement, from
  0.40 to 0.61 by day and from 0.46 to 0.62 by night. Rows that moved less are placed
  worse.

## 5. Deviations and incidents

1. **The share of the fall** (prediction row 5) is not defined by the directive. The
   exact decomposition of a product is the log split,
   `ln(ceiling_1 / ceiling_10) / ln(F1_1 / F1_10)`, which gives 0.28 (day) and 0.26
   (night). The plain ratio of differences, `(ceiling_1 - ceiling_10) /
   (F1_1 - F1_10)`, gives 0.45 and 0.41. Both use the medians over recordings of
   Table 1 and are in `report.json#/share_of_fall_of_F1_affine_accounted_for_by_ceiling`.
   The prediction holds on the first and fails on the second.
2. **"Median" in Tables 3 and 5.** Section 6 sets the aggregation of directive 001
   for every table, so the table values are medians over recordings. The pooled-row
   medians are next to them in the JSON. Table 5's smallest key bin has too few rows
   per recording for any recording to enter, so its pooled medians are printed in
   brackets.
3. **Table 4(a) recordings.** Some bins have few recordings (3 to 19), because a
   recording needs 20 rows in a bin at one target. The number entering is printed
   next to each `affine` value.
4. **Reference reports for acceptance check 3** (`results/gp/report.json` and
   `results/af/report.json`) were copied to the host next to the outputs. They are
   committed aggregates, not per-row files or data.

## 6. Questions for the director

1. **Slow objects.** Table 4(a) shows that rows with a small displacement at long
   leads are placed worst (`affine`, 0.40 to 0.56 below 10 px at m = 10). Slow
   objects also have `G` = 0 in directive 002 and the lowest `gamma` in directive 003,
   Table 4. Is a
   shorter group for slow objects, or a refreshed key, the change the next codec
   should test first?
2. **Counts in the outer growth bins.** A codec that scales the number of regenerated
   events by the box-area ratio would raise the ceiling from 0.76 to 0.85 toward the
   0.92 of steady objects. Placement, however, carries more of the loss. Is a count
   correction worth a parameter?
