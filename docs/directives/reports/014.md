## 6. Deviations and notes

- **Δt/τ = 0.25.** Section 11 asks for the fidelities at `dt / tau` = 0.25, which the reference grid
  of `ec.bridge` (Appendix A) does not hold. Section 3 gives 0.2 and 0.3 instead. Nothing was
  added to the grid.
- **A launch error.** The first launch on `cnt` backgrounded a `cd` together with the bridge job,
  so the suite and the slices did not start. The bridge itself ran correctly. The other two were
  launched again in the right directory. Nothing was written by the failed launch.
- **PDF bytes.** `fig_bits_all.pdf` and `fig_recon.pdf` differ from `76b182b` only in the
  `/CreationDate` of the PDF. Without it they are byte-identical, and so are their PNG and JSON
  files. `fig_limits` gained the gain panel of Appendix C, so its PDF and PNG are new drawings.
- **Fast module unused.** `ec/blockcodec_fast.py` (commit `7447cc1`) was not used. This directive
  runs no group.
