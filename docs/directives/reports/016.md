every configuration of the tuning grid. The criterion therefore freezes the configuration that predicts
least, and at that configuration it sends almost every event:
- its fidelity is at least 0.999;

## 7. Deviations and notes

1. **Read concurrency.** Section 8 says "read one recording at a time". DSEC was read one sequence at a
   time throughout, under the read lock and memory wait of directive 009. eTraM files are a few GB, and up
   to six eTraM reads ran at once (`READ_SLOTS` of directive 007). I read the sentence as being about the
   memory risk of the DSEC reads. If it meant both datasets, the eTraM part of the run did not follow it.
2. **Scheduler changes during the run.** None of them changed what a group receives or how it is computed.
   1. In Stage T, DSEC tasks filled the worker slots while queuing for the single DSEC read. I stopped the
      run, capped DSEC at six slots, and resumed it. The 36 task outputs written before were kept: same
      task code, under commit `5ab61cc`, and later ones under `6ea2e0e`. A first stop attempt also killed
      its own `pkill` shell, so the scheduler was relaunched once more.
   2. In Stage R, each DSEC task re-read its whole sequence for three groups. For the large sequences that
      took 800 s per read against 220 s of compute. After 198 of the 405 DSEC tasks, the remaining 20
      sequences were run with one read per sequence (`fb_run.py seqsR`, `ecee4ab`). That path cuts each
      group's events out of the single read and runs the tasks in forked processes; reads stay one at a
      time.
   3. Before using it, `thun_00_a` (2 tasks, 6 groups, already done) was recomputed through it. Its
      outputs were identical in every field except the timing fields.
3. **Seed of the random subset.** Section 7 says to draw the subset "per window with
   `np.random.default_rng(20261016 + w)`". I used `w` = the index of the window drawn, w + m, one generator
   per window. The subset is coded for the four windows m = 0..3 of the group, so that its bits are per
   true event of the group like REMAKE's.
4. **Aggregation choices** (`report.json#/aggregation`):
   - `st_us` of a recording is the median over its groups.
   - The motion error of a recording is the median over squares where both sets have events. "No event" is
     the share of squares where the scheme has none.
   - Bits of a recording are summed over its groups before the ratio.
5. **Boxes.** The annotation frame nearest the window centre is taken per track, with its raw box (not
   interpolated). The box is clipped to the sensor and kept with at least 50 true events of the window.
6. **REMAKE's records** for `translation_Q` and `static_Q` were present in every kept per-group file, so
   both ablations were run.
7. **Fast codec (outside this directive).** During this directive the author authorized, outside the
   directive scheme, the check of directive 007, Section 6, for `ec/blockcodec_fast.py`. It passed:
   - all 20 Stage 0 groups of 007 and 009 are equal in every field, records included;
   - all 96 Stage 0 streams are equal (`results/fast/stage0_check.json`, `9aff7a0`).

   From `9aff7a0` the runners of 007, 009 and 013 reach the codec through `ec/codec.py`, fast by default.
   This directive evaluated no REMAKE group. It used `ec.blockcodec` only to decode and reconstruct records,
   and to summarize the kept sums, so no number here depends on that change.
