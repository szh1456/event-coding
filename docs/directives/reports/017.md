All five acceptance checks pass. The DSEC-Detection labels sit on the event clock once the event file's
`t_offset` is subtracted. The two datasets differ sharply:

- **eTraM.** Most of REMAKE's regions touch an annotated box: 86% by day, 65% at night. They hold most of
  the key events in boxes.
- **DSEC.** 12.5% of the regions touch a box, and 10% of the key events lie in boxes. The boxes that hold
  at least 50 events still have 95% of their events in active blocks.
- **Precision.** On every grid it rises with Δt/τ, but stays below the Eq. (binfid) prediction for an exact
  map once Δt/τ ≥ 1/4.

## 1. What ran

was running.

As stored, the label times lie about 5.6 × 10¹⁰ µs past the event times (`t_offset`), so no label falls
in a key window. The convention used is therefore **minus `t_offset`**. Its median, 2.12, is above the
threshold of 2, though not by much. The labels come about every 50 ms (20 Hz).

## 8. Deviations and notes

1. **Downloads.** The archive was downloaded three times. Two of my extraction scripts failed: a quoting
   error, then a path match that missed the `train/` prefix. Both times the temporary copy was deleted
   before anything was kept. The kept files come from the third download, whose SHA-256 is recorded.
2. **`blocks.json`** is 202 MB, above GitHub's file limit, so it is committed as `blocks.json.gz`
   (53 MB, gzip -9). The content is unchanged.
3. **Cache for directive 018.** While reading each DSEC sequence, the tasks also wrote every group's events
   of `[(w − 3) T0, (w + 11) T0)` to `/data/<user>/event_coding/ds/cache/<sequence>.npz` (96 GB). That range
   contains the range directive 018 needs, so 018 does not read the 36 sequences again (about 1 hour of
   serialized reads). Nothing of this directive depends on the cache.
4. **Decoders.** Records were decoded through the decoders of `ec.blockcodec_fast` (`ec.codec.use_fast_decoders`).
   Those decoders equal the reference on every Stage 0 stream (`results/fast/stage0_check.json`), and
   `match_equals_kept` holds for every group.
5. **The median DSEC spacing.** The median τ on DSEC is 9,999 µs, close to 10 ms. This is reported as
   measured; I did not look into its cause.
6. **Table 2's speed** is that of the map at m = 3 over 3 windows, as `ec.regions` defines it. Static means
   |d| ≤ 1 px for m = 1..3 and no shape parameter.
