## 9. Deviations and notes

1. **Fidelity sums.** Importing `hd_run` (D-021-2) wraps `ec.hevcdiag.fidelities` to also return its
   summed terms, as in 019. F and Fsnr are unchanged; the tables pool recordings from those sums.
2. **Fidelity targets.** `b_at_F` is interpolated per recording, between the first two consecutive QPs (in
   QP order) that bracket the target. Recordings whose ladder does not bracket it are NaN and counted.
3. **Coarse tasks.** The task scores of the coarse part run on the lossy modes at both placements and are
   pooled over the groups of a recording, as in 018 and 019.
4. **`model.json`** keeps the per-mode totals of each scene and grid, without per-picture rows, as
   Section 9 asks.
5. **Network.** One ssh connection to `cnt` failed during the reduction and the retry succeeded. Nothing
   was rerun.
