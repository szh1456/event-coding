## 4. Deviations and incidents

1. **Locating the annotation files.** `docs/DATA.md` gives the annotation root but not
   its subdirectory, and none of five guessed paths existed. I listed the *directory*
   names below the root, with `find -maxdepth 4 -type d` and no file names:
   `train/eight_class_annotations_train`, `val/eight_class_annotations_val`,
   `test_count/eight_class_annotations_test`, `zips`. After that, every path was built
   from an identifier and checked with `os.path.isfile`. No file in `val/`,
   `test_count/` or `zips/` was listed, opened or hashed. `ec.annotations.find_annotation`,
   whose recursive glob would read every file name below the root, is not used.
2. **pytest on `cnt`.** The system pytest 6.2.5 aborts collection on an `anyio` plugin
   in the user site-packages (`ModuleNotFoundError: _pytest.scope`). The suite runs with
   `-p no:anyio`. No test is affected.
3. **Packages.** zstandard 0.25.0, numba 0.60.0 and llvmlite 0.43.0 were installed with
   `pip --target ~/prjs/event_coding/pylib --no-deps`, so the host's numpy 1.26.4 stays
   in use. numba is installed but nothing uses it.
4. **Event slices in Stage M.** A shard reads its recording with `read_events` and then
   keeps only the events in `[min t_first, max t_last]` of its tracks.
   `evaluate_instant` returns `outside_track_life` before reading any event whenever a
   window leaves the track's life, so the results are identical. This was checked on
   the 180 cost-probe rows of two recordings, run once on full arrays and once on
   slices: every output agrees exactly (largest difference 0).
5. **Cost.** The full-run estimate was 296 CPU-hours, with a 95% bootstrap interval of
   246 to 365 (`preflight.json#/cost`). The author authorized the run on the point
   estimate, with the instruction not to compete with the companion project's job. The
   run used 315.4 CPU-hours. The cost model takes one mean for every box over
   30,000 px². Under it the two last shards were estimated at 16 and 10 minutes; they
   hold boxes of 553,000 and 462,000 px² and took 68 and 39 minutes. Measured
   cost per instant (six settings, one core) by box area, from the probe:

   | box area, px² | sampled instants | mean s | generated instants |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | under 1,000 | 2 | 0.52 | 2,164 |
   | 1,000-3,000 | 46 | 1.41 | 17,655 |
   | 3,000-10,000 | 84 | 3.92 | 26,495 |
   | 10,000-30,000 | 66 | 14.7 | 17,173 |
   | 30,000+ | 42 | 66.2 (max 431) | 10,297 |

   A profile of one instant (72,700 px², 281 px/s) puts 57% of the time in
   `gaussian_filter` on the 1/8-px grid and 19% in the time-sampled normalizer.
6. **Choices where the directive is silent**, all fixed before any Stage M number was
   seen:
   - P3 shifts of ±33 ms are ±33,333 us. The window is the first 60 s of events.
     A frame enters if its shifted window `[tau, tau + 33,333 us)` lies inside that
     window. A box covers the integer pixels `ceil(x) .. floor(x + w)`, matching the
     inclusive test of `in_box_mask`. Area is the union of the frame's boxes.
   - P4 speed is the median over a track's evaluation instants of
     `|track.velocity(t_e, 100 ms)|`. P4 area is the median `w * h` over the track's
     frames.
   - The speed stratum of a row uses `track.velocity(t_e, T1)` of that row's setting,
     the same value as `speed_label`. The overlap stratum uses that row's support
     rectangle against the boxes of the other tracks alive at `t_e`.
   - `Delta` is formed per instant and then medianed. The "share of instants with
     `b_px` at the smallest bandwidth" and the share of instants with the speed ratio
     in [0.8, 1.25] are means over a recording's instants, then medianed over
     recordings. A median of a 0/1 indicator says nothing.
   - The Table 7 share is (in-box events of the future windows of scored instants)
     over (the same for all generated instants). "In-box" means inside the track's
     interpolated box dilated by `margin_px`.
7. **Reference ledger.** No new external source backs any claim. The companion's
   `objects_provenance.json` hashes served as an integrity check of the annotation
   files only. `docs/REFERENCE_LEDGER.md` is outside the executor's paths, so it is
   unchanged. The entry `etram_eight_class_annotations` already covers the files.

## 5. Questions for the director

1. **The bandwidth set is censored from above.** `BANDWIDTHS_PX` stops at 1 px, and in
   stratum H at (100, 0) the chosen `b_px` sits at that maximum for 100% of the
   `static` instants, 64 / 71% of the `label` instants and 42 / 56% of the `cmax`
   instants (day / night). At D = 300 ms it is 94-98% for `label` and `cmax`.
   Every gain is therefore a lower bound, and `Delta` is a difference of two lower
   bounds whose bias is not signed. `static` is the more censored model, so a wider set
   could lower `Delta`. The decision sits on this. Should the next measurement extend
   the bandwidth set (for example 2 and 4 px) before, or together with, a scale-aware
   warp or a finer grid? Counts per bandwidth, by model and setting, come from the
   per-instant files on the host.
2. `eps(cmax)` is 0.02 to 0.03, against a predicted 0.2 to 0.7. With the floor nearly
   unused, the unexplained events are absorbed by the template's width instead (wide
   `b_px`, `tau_eq` of 4.5 to 7 ms). Is `eps` still the quantity that should carry the
   "share the template does not explain" in later directives, or should a fixed-width
   variant be reported next to it?
3. `Delta` is larger at D = 33 ms than at D = 0 (2.30 / 2.10 against 2.04 / 1.63),
   because `gain(static)` falls faster with the lead than `gain(cmax)`. A causal coder
   always predicts across some lead. Should the decision rule be read at the lead such
   a coder would have, rather than at D = 0?
4. The cost scales with box area. If the next directive keeps the 1/8-px grid and adds
   bandwidths or warps, an accelerated `predictive_cost` is worth building first: direct
   kernel sums at the needed points instead of filtering the whole grid, and sharing the
   template across settings with the same `T1`. It would still have to pass the gate and
   reproduce the reference to 1e-6. Should it be authorized?
