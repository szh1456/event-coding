## 5. Deviations and incidents

1. **Scheduler bookkeeping (my bug, results unaffected).** The run driver at
   `12032a1` looked for its outputs under an unpadded name (`rows_<rid>_1.npz`),
   while each task writes `rows_<rid>_001.npz`. It therefore logged every finished
   task as `FAIL ... rc=0` and launched it once more. The second launch found its
   output and returned at once, without reading or computing, so every shard was
   computed exactly once. The run was left untouched: a restart could have computed
   running shards twice into the same file. Completeness was verified independently:
   700 task records, and acceptance check 3. The fix is in `98aa154`. In the same
   commit `sync_cnt.sh` now refuses to sync while any `mp_`, `rg_` or `gp_` worker
   runs; before, it checked only for `mp_` workers.
2. **Reducer dry run.** Before it was committed, `scripts/gp_reduce.py` was copied
   once to the host mirror and run on the first 15 shards into a scratch directory,
   which was then deleted. No running worker used that file. The final reduction
   ran after a regular sync at `98aa154`.
3. **Stage S in the Stage C pass.** Every recording has a shard 0 in directive
   001's plan, and that task computed Stage S on the full event array before the
   events were cut to the shard's tracks. A window `U_j` is taken when
   `[j s, j s + 33,333 us)` lies inside `[first event, last event]`, that is when
   `j s + 33,332 <= t_last`.
4. **Shards and event slices,** as in directive 002. Acceptance check 4 confirms
   exact agreement with directive 002 on every shared row.
5. **Read slots.** At most 6 tasks read a recording at once, against 3 in directive
   002. Peak memory use stayed under 150 GB of 251.
6. **Choices where the directive is silent,** fixed before any number:
   - Table 2's "median distance" is the per-recording median of the per-row
     Euclidean distance between `(aligned_dx, aligned_dy)` and `(start_dx,
     start_dy)`, then the median over recordings. The share of rows that differ is
     a per-recording mean.
   - A group quantity enters a recording's median only for instants that have that
     group, and a recording enters with at least 20 such instants.
   - Acceptance check 7's zero group sets `f1` to 0 along with `n_pred`, as in
     `tests/test_gop_gate.py`.
   - Stage S medians carry bootstrap intervals with seed 20261010.
7. **Cost.** 62.3 CPU-hours against a projection of 50.4. The probe again sampled
   no instant with a box over 100,000 px² (1,344 instants). Within the limit of 120.

## 6. Questions for the director

1. **Sent displacement against extrapolated velocity.** At K = 4, `aligned` gains
   only 0.07 to 0.09 in `gamma` over `cmax`, and 0.57 to 0.74 at K = 11. The
   displacement costs 24 bits per window, against about 27 bits per key event. Does
   the frozen codec keep the per-window displacement, or send it only beyond a lead
   at which the extrapolation error exceeds a pixel (from `m` = 4 on, Table 2)?
2. **Group size for the second dataset.** `gamma` grows with K while the fidelity
   falls: 0.71 at K = 4, 0.54 at K = 11 (day). The rule fixes no K for validation.
   Should the validation directive fix K = 4, or carry K as a parameter judged by
   the downstream task?
3. **Slow and non-vehicle objects.** `gamma(aligned)` at K = 4 is 1.18 to 1.49 for
   "other" classes below 100 px/s, and 1.52 to 1.86 for slow vehicles. The two
   refinements held back in Section 14 (a time offset per window, a coded
   innovation) target exactly these. Should they be measured on the development
   recordings before or after the second dataset?
